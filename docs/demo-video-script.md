# Demo video script

**Kab Tak? for Man Matters** · about 3 minutes · screen recording of the phone view with voice-over

Record the [live app](https://dsrivastava-py.github.io/manmatters/) on a phone, or in a browser window about 390px wide. Start with no saved data (Settings, then **Clear all my data**). Have the Part 1 document open for the first scene.

| Time | On screen | What to say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:15 | Your face or the title slide | "Hi, I'm Devansh Srivastava from Kirori Mal College. For the Mosaic Fellowship, I spoke to Man Matters hair-loss customers and built Kab Tak?, which means 'How long?'" |
| 0:15 to 0:40 | Part 1 document: patterns and problem statement | "The customers I spoke to [Fill in: say what most of them said]. Online, the most common theme was the same: people can't tell if it's working. They panic at the early shed, quit at the plateau, or keep paying for a plan that isn't working. Man Matters already tracks the routine. What's missing is someone explaining what you're seeing." |
| 0:40 to 1:00 | Start screen; tap **1 month ago** | "There's no login and no download. You enter one date, the day you started." |
| 1:00 to 1:25 | Your Week: week number, timeline, next milestone | "It tells you where you are: week 5, shed settling. It shows what's commonly reported right now and, the reason for the name, when things should change, with a real date." |
| 1:25 to 1:50 | Tap **I'm shedding more**; pick "A bit more than usual"; show result | "If your hair is falling more, three taps tell you if that's commonly reported at your week, what to do, and when to check again." |
| 1:50 to 2:05 | Run again, tick **Chest symptoms** | "But if there's a warning sign, it never reassures you. It says stop and see a doctor, and for chest symptoms it shows the emergency number." |
| 2:05 to 2:35 | Settings: set start date about 12 weeks ago; Checkpoint: answer, show result and "Why this result" | "At weeks 12 and 24 there's an honest checkpoint. It gives a clear answer: on track, early signs, fix your routine, or talk to a doctor. It shows why. Being honest when it isn't working is what makes 'on track' believable." |
| 2:35 to 2:45 | **I slipped**, tap 10 days | "Missed some days? It explains lapse versus plateau, so a missed fortnight isn't mistaken for failure." |
| 2:45 to 2:55 | Switch to Hinglish; turn on dark mode; open **Summary for my doctor** | "It works in English and Hinglish, offline, in dark mode, and makes a summary you can show your doctor. Nothing leaves your phone." |
| 2:55 to 3:05 | Your face or the end slide with the link | "Kab Tak? works alongside the Man Matters app: the app tracks the routine, Kab Tak? explains the results. Thank you." |

**Tips**

- Keep each scene short and let the screen do the talking.
- Do not show any customer names or phone numbers in the video.
- Fill in the one line in the second scene with what your customers actually said.
